# Backdrops

The home screen and the screensaver show 24 landscape photos that come with
Rozan. Each one is public domain or CC0. The credits below name every
photo's author, licence and source, and Rozan's settings show the same
credits under "Backdrop photos".

## Licences

Every photo comes from Wikimedia Commons, and its licence was checked on
Commons. Two kinds of file qualify:

- Works of US federal agencies (National Park Service, US Forest Service),
  tagged public domain on Commons.
- Photos published on Unsplash before June 2017, while Unsplash released
  photos under CC0 1.0. Commons hosts copies tagged CC0 with a link to the
  Unsplash page.

Files tagged CC BY, CC BY-SA, GFDL or with an unknown licence were not
used. Credit is recorded for every photo even where the licence does not
require it.

## Processing

The photos are natural landscapes in a mix of colours, seasons and times of
day. Each original was cropped to 16:9, converted to sRGB, scaled down to
1920x1080, stripped of its metadata and saved as a JPEG. The 24 files take
6.9 MiB.

They are in `app/launcher/src/main/assets/backdrops/`, with a
`manifest.json` that records each photo's title, author, licence, source
page, original file URL and the SHA-256 of the processed JPEG.
[DEVELOPMENT.md](DEVELOPMENT.md#backdrop-photos) has the selection rules,
the processing command and each photo's crop.
